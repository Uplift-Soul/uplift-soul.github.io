INST Privacy

Gaming

The short version

INVST Gaming is a free dashboard of public Twitch viewing data. There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is sold on. We count visits anonymously so we know the site is being used, and we only use Google Analytics if you say yes.

Questions or requests: invstgaming@gmail.com.

Visit counting — always on, no cookies

We use Umami (Umami Cloud) to count visits. It stores nothing on your device — no cookies, no local storage — so it doesn't need your consent. It records:

  • which pages are viewed, and the site or search that linked you here
  • your country, browser, operating system and device type
  • clicks on the site's own controls (for example chart range buttons, or Accept / Decline on the privacy banner)

You aren't identified. Umami recognises a visit using an anonymous code made from your IP address and browser details, which changes every day, so you can't be followed from one day to the next; your IP address itself isn't stored. We rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how much the site is used.

Google Analytics — only if you accept

If you choose Accept, Google Analytics 4 sets cookies (_ga, _ga_…) so it can tell visits apart and see how the dashboard is used: pages viewed, approximate location, device, and how you use the charts and search. Google Analytics doesn't store IP addresses, advertising features are switched off, and the data is kept for 14 months. The legal basis is your consent.

Before you choose, or if you decline, Google's script still loads but sets no cookies. It may send Google basic signals without identifiers (such as that a page was viewed), which aren't used to recognise you.

Your choice

We remember your Accept / Decline choice in your browser's storage (invst-consent), so the banner doesn't keep asking. That's its only use. You can change your mind at any time — declining after accepting switches Google Analytics off and deletes its cookies.

Other services

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • The dashboard's data comes from Twitch's public API and IGDB. It's about games and public streams, not about you.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its use. Because the visit counting is anonymous, we usually can't link any of it back to you. Email invstgaming@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
